Student organization, University of Central Florida

I started UCF's first student organization for product management.

Loop exists to build a talent pipeline from campus into PM roles, through workshops, case competitions, and speaker sessions. I founded it, recruited the officer team, and ran it as president.

My role

How I designed the org, recruited leaders, and delivered outcomes.

Vision and strategy

Identified the gap in PM education on campus and led career development projects to close it.

Program design: weekly workshops, project sprints, and a speaker series.

Leadership and operations

Recruited officers, defined roles, and managed KnightConnect registration, branding, and comms.

Team: VP, Product Dev, Ideathon Coordinator, Technical Dev, Treasurer, Secretary.

Programming and outcomes

Designed leadership development programs on product lifecycle, user research, and AI tools, and scaled membership on the strength of what students walked away with.

Shipping the club website
