Personal product  ·  2026, in development

Hindsight

Reviews tell you what a place was like. They rarely tell you what you should have done instead. Hindsight collects the second thing.

How it works

After a trip, travelers log what they wish they had done differently. Entries cluster by how many people said the same thing, so the signal is the count rather than any one opinion, and the next traveler reads the pattern before they go.

Where it stands

PRD drafted, corpus seeded from public travel forum content and growing as people add entries. Images resolve automatically from tags rather than being uploaded.

Personal product  ·  2026

Arrival

Most language apps assume you want to learn a language. Before a trip, you do not. You want the twenty exchanges you are actually going to have, in the order you will have them.

How it works

Arrival takes your destination, how long the trip is, what you plan to do while you are there, how long you have before departure, and how much time you can study each day. From that it generates a plan that prioritizes the words, phrases, and situations you will genuinely run into.
